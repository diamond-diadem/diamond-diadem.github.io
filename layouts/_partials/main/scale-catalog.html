Shared by the codes catalog and the tutorials catalog (codes-catalog.html, documentation-catalog.html), which only differ in the pages they list. A ruler opens the catalog: it names the scales with their typical length and links to each group, and each group repeats it in miniature to show where it sits along it. A scale with nothing listed stays on the ruler as a hollow stop. Each cell is labelled with its tag (see main/code-tags.html). Items with no tagline, or an unknown scale or tag, are reported at build time. When the items carry two tags or more, a filter follows the ruler: one toggle per tag, all on at first, that shows or hides its cells (catalog-filter.js, which also hides the groups left empty and updates their counts).
