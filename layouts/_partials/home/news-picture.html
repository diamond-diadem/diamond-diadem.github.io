{{- /* Picture heading a news card, cropped to a common shape and offered in two widths. It only illustrates the card, whose title says what it is about, so its text alternative is empty. @param {page} page A news page, whose backgroundImage names the picture. @param {string} sizes Width of the picture in the layout, for the browser to pick a file. @param {bool} eager Whether the picture shows on arrival, so it loads first instead of lazily. */ -}} {{- with .page.Params.backgroundImage -}} {{- with resources.Get . -}} {{- $picture := .Fill "640x360 Center webp q80" -}} {{- $srcset := slice (printf "%s 640w" $picture.RelPermalink) -}} {{- if ge .Width 1280 -}} {{- $srcset = $srcset | append (printf "%s 1280w" (.Fill "1280x720 Center webp q75").RelPermalink) -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}}
